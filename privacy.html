LEGAL & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Appigo (https://appigo.site) respects visitor privacy and is committed to transparency regarding how information is handled when you browse our website.

1. Information We Collect

Appigo does not require visitors to create an account or submit personal registration details to browse game guides or play embedded browser games. Standard server and CDN logs (such as anonymized IP addresses, browser type, device type, and referring pages) may be processed automatically to maintain security, prevent abuse, and measure site performance.

2. Cookies and Advertising Partners

We may use cookies and similar technologies for site analytics and to display advertisements through third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense and authorized partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.

3. Embedded Third-Party Games

Some games available on Appigo are loaded via secure iframes hosted by third-party game developers or distribution networks. When you interact with an embedded game, that third-party provider may store local game progress (such as high scores in browser LocalStorage) or collect technical telemetry governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Appigo does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through our contact channels, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Page or email us at support@appigo.site.
